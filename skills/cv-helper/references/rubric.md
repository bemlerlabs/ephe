# Quality Rubric

1. Every position: impact instead of duty. Format "verb + what + measurable result" ("Reduced deploy time by 40% through CI automation"). Mark missing numbers as [X] placeholders and ask the user for them.
2. ATS-safe: common section titles (Experience, Education, Skills), no tables, icons or columns in the core, mirror keywords from the target job ad (if provided).
3. Order by career stage: 3 years of experience or less -> education first; otherwise experience first.
4. For an export request: create_pdf with template "report" - one to two pages, no frills.
5. Honesty: do not embellish anything the user cannot prove.
6. Ground every number/path on real tool output; no simulated output without a returned path.
